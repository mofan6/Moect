WeChat built-in dice and rock-paper-scissors result artwork

Artwork copyright belongs to Tencent and its respective rights holders.
Moect is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tencent.

The nine original PNG result sprites are bundled without modifying their bytes.
They represent the final result, not a newly generated or randomized animation.
Their original WeChat MD5 identities were checked against local message records;
SHA-256 fingerprints are recorded in ui/wechat-game-emojis.json.
